  • Encrypted Vault Persistence: All client records use enc:v1: salt-based keystream encryption with SHA-256 tamper verification.
  • Biometric Passkeys: Hardware WebAuthn authentication (Touch ID, Face ID, YubiKey) ensures secure director access.

5. Step-by-Step Interactive Tutorials

Tutorial 1: Creating a Complete Case from First Call to Form AP-47

  1. Log in to the Director Backoffice with your Passkey or 6-digit PIN.
  2. Click "+ New Case Intake" in the top navigation bar.
  3. Enter decedent's full name, date of death, and location (e.g., Harlem Hospital).
  4. Enter informant contact details and click "Save & Create Golden Record".
  5. Click "Open Contract Builder" and select "Traditional Harlem Heritage Package".
  6. Select the Batesville 18-Gauge Auburn Sunset casket.
  7. Add cash advances: 10 Certified Death Certificates ($150), Clergy ($350), NYC Burial Permit ($40).
  8. Click "Generate Form AP-47" ➔ "Dispatch via DocuSign".

Tutorial 2: Designing & Exporting a Memorial Booklet for Press

  1. From the Case Record, click "Memorial Program Studio".
  2. Select 8-Page Booklet and Harlem Burgundy theme.
  3. Upload cover portrait; fine-tune crop and positioning.
  4. Use "AI Assist" to refine the obituary narrative.
  5. Populate Order of Service and Pallbearer rosters.
  6. Click "Export CMYK Press-Ready PDF" to download 300 DPI print files.

7. Compliance & Legal Hierarchy (NYS PHL § 4201)

Priority Relationship Tier Required Verification / Affidavit
Tier 1 Designated Written Agent NYS PHL § 4201 form signed prior to death with 2 witnesses.
Tier 2 Surviving Spouse Valid marriage; no final divorce decree or legal separation.
Tier 3 Domestic Partner Registered NYC domestic partnership or joint financial interdependence.
Tier 4 Adult Children Majority consensus; requires sibling waiver for non-signing children.
Tier 5 Surviving Parents Either surviving parent with legal kinship standing.
Tier 6 Adult Siblings Majority consensus of surviving brothers and sisters.
Tier 7 Estate Guardian Letters of Guardianship from New York Surrogate's Court.

8. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: How do family members access their private portal?
A: Directors click "Send Family Portal Access SMS" on the Case Overview. The family receives a direct SMS link with a secure PIN.

Q: What if I make a typo in the decedent's name?
A: Edit the Golden Record master profile; the Discrepancy Engine will automatically update Form AP-47, EDRS, and Memorial Programs simultaneously.

Q: How do we sync transactions to QuickBooks Online?
A: Open the Integrations Command Center, navigate to the QuickBooks tab, and click "Sync Ledger". Invoices and cash advances balance automatically.
