## 🛡️ The 7 Core "Mistake-Proof" Guardrails

### 1. The Discrepancy & Kinship Guardrail Engine
* **The Problem:** An estranged relative attempts to arrange a service, or a family member signs without legal priority under **NYS PHL § 4201**, exposing the home to family disputes or court injunctions.
* **The Guardrail:** The system automatically cross-references the informant’s legal relationship against the 7-tier NYS Statutory Hierarchy (1. Designated Written Agent ➔ 2. Surviving Spouse ➔ 3. Domestic Partner ➔ 4. Adult Children Consensus ➔ 5. Parents ➔ 6. Siblings ➔ 7. Estate Guardian). If a priority conflict or missing sibling waiver exists, the system flashes a **Statutory Kinship Alert** and provides the exact legal affidavit required.

### 2. NYC DOHMH eVital / EDRS Data Scrubbing
* **The Problem:** Submitting a death certificate with a missing Mother’s Maiden Name, mismatched Social Security Number, or incorrect place of death delays the burial permit and halts the cemetery or crematory committal.
* **The Guardrail:** Before generating the EDRS payload, the system audits all 18 mandatory vital fields. If a required field is blank or incomplete, it blocks submission with a direct prompt to the director. Once verified, it generates the official **72-Hour Burial/Transit Permit** payload with zero re-typing.

### 3. Form AP-47 Exact Mathematical Itemization (NYS Article 34)
* **The Problem:** Manual math errors on the 12 spaces of Form AP-47 (Statement of Goods and Services Selected) or incorrect sales tax calculation on caskets violate NYS Department of Health regulations.
* **The Guardrail:** Every line item—from Basic Services of Funeral Director and Staff to Embalming, Casket Selection, Limousines, and Cash Advances—is dynamically computed from our registered General Price List (GPL). Total balances, cash advances, and payments match down to the exact penny.

### 4. DocuSign NYS ESRA Signature Verification
* **The Problem:** Families forget to sign an embalming authorization, or paper copies are misplaced between the arrangement office and prep room.
* **The Guardrail:** All statutory forms (Form AP-47, Embalming Consent, Batesville Warranty, PHL § 4201 Affidavits) are bundled into an electronic envelope. Signers authenticate via 2-factor SMS OTP codes from their smartphones. Completed PDFs are permanently sealed with tamper-proof digital certificates in the case record.

### 5. Cash Advance Pass-Through Check Printer
* **The Problem:** Rushing on the morning of a service to write manual paper checks for clergy, organists, and certified death certificates leads to checkbook discrepancies, unrecorded cash advances, or forgotten vendor payments.
* **The Guardrail:** With 1 tap, the system generates pre-formatted, MICR-encoded **JPMorgan Chase Pass-Through Checks** for the exact statutory amounts (e.g., Clergy $350, Organist $250, NYC DOHMH transcripts $15/copy). It instantly logs delivery by Director Jason Benta and synchronizes directly with QuickBooks Online.

### 6. Livery Fleet 10-Hour Lock & Driver SMS Dispatch
* **The Problem:** Last-minute changes to cortege pickup times or missed communication with limousine chauffeurs cause cortege delays and stress for the family.
* **The Guardrail:** Livery routes lock 10 hours prior to service to prevent accidental dispatch conflicts. Drivers receive automated cellular SMS notifications containing pickup addresses, passenger counts, and gatehouse instructions for Woodlawn, Calverton, or Pinelawn.

### 7. S3 Golden Record Vault with SHA-256 Hashes
* **The Problem:** Paper case files get lost in file cabinets or damaged over time.
* **The Guardrail:** Every high-res memorial photograph, signed contract, Living Voice audio recording, and transit permit is cryptographically hashed and backed up in real time to AWS S3 and Supabase cloud storage. Records are searchable in seconds from any iPad, iPhone, or office computer.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions & Reassurances for Staff

#### Q1: "What if I make a typo or enter the wrong information?"
> **Answer:** The system does not lock a case until the director explicitly approves it. If an informant provides updated information (such as an amended death date or middle name), the **Discrepancy Engine** automatically flags where the change needs to happen and updates all connected documents with a single click.

#### Q2: "What if the internet goes down at 630 St. Nicholas Ave?"
> **Answer:** The platform is equipped with **offline-first local caching**. All case forms, active contracts, and director HUD views remain accessible on local iPads and computers even during an internet disruption. As soon as connectivity returns, changes synchronize seamlessly to the cloud.

#### Q3: "Will older family members find the Family Portal difficult to use?"
> **Answer:** The family portal was designed with large, clear fonts, high-contrast buttons, and zero confusing tech terminology. Whether an elderly family member uses an iPhone, Android, or desktop computer, everything—from reading the obituary to viewing the 4K sanctuary webcast—is accessible in 1 click without requiring complex passwords.

#### Q4: "Does this replace our personal telephone calls to families?"
> **Answer:** Absolutely not. Telephone calls, in-person handshakes, and personal presence remain the hallmark of Benta’s service. This platform handles the administrative background tasks so that you have *more* time to speak with families, answer their questions, and provide comfort.
